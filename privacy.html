What this site records,
and what it never does.

We count visits to learn what works on this site and which companies are interested in what we do. Here is all of it, plainly.

What we record

  • The pages you view, how long they stay on screen, how far you scroll, and the buttons you press.
  • Where your visit came from: the link you followed, any campaign tag on it, or the site that sent you.
  • Your device type, browser, system, screen size, language and time zone, as your browser reports them.
  • Your approximate city, state and country, and, if you visit from a company's network, the name of that company. Your internet address is used for this lookup and is never stored.
  • The answers you tap when making a profile. Anything you type is recorded only if you send it to us.
  • Anything you choose to send us, such as a profile or a note.

What we never do

  • We never store your internet address, fingerprint your device, or follow you to other sites.
  • We never buy data to put a name to an anonymous visitor.
  • We never sell or share what we record.

How it is kept

Your browser keeps a few small notes for this site: whether you accepted, the date you last visited, and the current visit, which ends after 30 minutes without activity. They hold no ID that identifies you. If you accept, Microsoft Clarity also records how pages are used, with anything typed hidden, so we can see where the site confuses people; it sets its own first-party cookies. The records are kept in our own database, which only we can read.

Your choice

Decline, and we still count the visit, without your location, company network, return date or Clarity. You can change your answer here.

Questions

Write to prism@clairvistapartners.com. Ask us to delete anything you sent, and we will.

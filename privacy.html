Privacy

We use first-party cookies, browser storage and Microsoft Clarity to understand how this site is used and to improve it. We record the pages you view and how you use them, the link or site that brought you here, your device and browser, your approximate location, and the company network you visit from, if any. Your internet address is used for that lookup and never stored.

When you make a profile, we keep the answers you tap; anything you type stays with you unless you send it. What you send us is kept so we can reply.

We don't sell or share any of it, and we don't track you on other sites.

Questions, or a request to delete something you sent: prism@clairvistapartners.com.
